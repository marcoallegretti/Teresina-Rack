## Power modes and budget boundaries

- Positive carrier input passes D1 to the internal positive rail; USB VBUS passes D5 to that rail. D1/D5 inhibit intentional reverse feed into carrier/USB. Reverse leakage remains: manufacturer p2 specifies40 µA maximum at15 V/25 °C; visual review corrects the text extractor's incorrect `mA` unit. Higher-temperature curves have greater leakage. This topology is not galvanic isolation or a zero-backfeed guarantee.
- Negative supply D2 orientation is correct for negative-rail reverse-polarity protection. A series diode cannot be credited as overvoltage clamping or surge protection.
- USB-only supplies the buck/MCU but cannot produce a regulated5 V with a 5 V LDO fed below5 V, nor the negative audio rail/reference. Official datasheet printedp2 explicitly allows partial USB power for firmware updates and says the codec will not function properly. Validate actual reset/DFU/memory behavior and current negotiation on hardware. Do not describe USB-only as full audio operation.
- With positive carrier power present, D5 reverse stress is roughly positive-rail minus VBUS; 17 V carrier with no USB is within the diode20 V continuous reverse rating, with limited transient margin. With reversed−17 V at positive carrier input while USB holds internal rail near+4.7 V, D1 sees about21.7 V, beyond20 V continuous reverse rating (30 V peak rating does not authorize sustained operation). Decide and validate the complete misconnection/transient contract.
- TPS62170 is rated500 mA **total** output. Internal MCU, SDRAM, QSPI, codec digital current and external A10 load share it. Official datasheet printedp2 describes500 mA as an absolute maximum output and marks it firmware dependent; do not turn that into guaranteed external500 mA on top of internal load. Compute peak/startup and workload-specific residual budget.
- LD1117 output800 mA is also shared by the codec analog supply, twelve MCP6004 amplifiers and external A6. At a representative protected11.7 V, dissipation is `6.7*Iout + 11.7*Iq`, where ST gives Iq5 mA typical/10 mA max. At100 mA, this is0.729..0.787 W. At800 mA,5.419..5.477 W. ST SOT223 reference θJA110 °C/W, θJC15 °C/W, max junction125 °C (p7) do not validate any particular PCB's heat spreading. At40 °C ambient the reference model permits only~0.773 W total before125 °C, illustrating why external800 mA cannot be promised continuously at12 V without a very different thermal solution. No unconditional current limit is approved from this reference calculation; characterize the actual copper/airflow/load and operate below the junction maximum with design margin.
- Near6 V carrier, even a correct LD1117 is close to dropout after D1 and filter drops. ST's guaranteed5 V table begins6.5 V at the IC. Equivalent minimum-input performance requires a supported power solution, not an assumption that absolute input limits guarantee precise5 V.

The official rev3 power sheet uses `SRP2010-2R2M` for its buck. Bourns manufacturer PDF p1 confirms2.2 µH±20%, DCR150mΩ, Irms1.7 A, Isat1.9 A with datasheet test definitions. It is a valid *candidate* reference part, not a footprint-compatible replacement for present1210 L1. TI p14 prescribes saturation above computed `Iout+ΔIL/2` plus approximately20% margin, and recommends3.3 µH at low inputs for full output current. Calculate using minimum effective inductance and actual frequency, then verify selected orderable part and placement. TI p13/p15 expects a supported LC pair, accounting for ±20% assumed bias/tolerance variation; distributing capacitors across the board does not substitute for a local output filter.

A0.65 mm-pitch array with0.15 mm drilled vias leaves0.50 mm between adjacent drill edges. One0.09 mm track plus two0.20 mm hole clearances consumes0.49 mm: only0.01 mm nominal spare before geometry/tolerance, and two tracks cannot fit. This simple corridor calculation proves neither success nor failure for the full fanout. Assess actual diagonal corridors, pads, anti-pads, voids and ground continuity with native DRC and drilling constraints.0.80 mm SDRAM has more local room but the actual crossovers, power delivery and timings still govern feasibility. Through-vias must cross every layer with appropriate keepouts and plane anti-pads.

The actual official1.6 mm/1 oz outer/0.5 oz inner stackup response includes19four-layer and17six-layer templates. Examples:

- **JLC04161H-3313:** outer copper0.035 mm; outer-to-plane prepreg0.09940 mm each side; L2/L3 copper0.0152 mm each; core1.265 mm. Good candidate for four layers with outer signal/power and two continuous inner ground references. Distribute power on outer copper with explicit budget; plane perforation/return-path review remains mandatory. A split power plane under unrelated fast signals is not automatically an acceptable reference.
- **JLC04161H-1080:** outer-to-plane0.07640 mm each side; core1.265 mm, same copper, another named four-layer option to evaluate for impedance/fanout.
- **JLC04161H-7628:** outer-to-plane0.21040 mm/core1.065 mm; materially different signal impedance/coupling, so generic1.6 mm assumptions are inadequate.
- **JLC06161H-3313:** outer-to-L2/L5 prepreg0.09940 mm, two0.55 mm cores, central2116prepreg0.1088 mm, same1 oz/0.5 oz copper. This supplies more copper layers but layer assignments still require continuous nearby references; it does not automatically fix return paths.

These are manufacturer-enabled response entries, not a saved quotation/order approval. The legacy official impedance page exposes4and6templates only; no exact8layer template was established during this review. Eight remains final fallback, to be sourced and independently reviewed **only after documented4and6failure**. Increasing layers will not correct wrong parts, bad power loops, pin-map errors or thermal limits.
